Accessing AOM 2.0
=================

AOM resources are region-specific and cannot be used across regions. Select a region before accessing AOM.

Constraints
-----------

-  To return to the AOM 1.0 console, choose **Back to 1.0** in the navigation pane of the AOM 2.0 console. To go to the AOM 2.0 console, choose **AOM 2.0** in the navigation pane of the AOM 1.0 console.

Procedure
---------

#. Log in to the management console.

#. Click |image1| in the upper left corner and select your desired region from the drop-down list.

#. Click |image2| on the left and choose **Application** > **Application Operations Management**. The AOM console is displayed.

   In the navigation pane, choose **AOM 2.0**. The AOM 2.0 console is displayed.


   .. figure:: /_static/images/en-us_image_0000002336872256.png
      :alt: **Figure 1** Going to the AOM 2.0 console

      **Figure 1** Going to the AOM 2.0 console

#. On the **Service Authorization** page that is displayed, read the *Authorization Statement* and select "I have read and agreed to the *Authorization Statement*".

#. Click **Subscribe and Authorize for Free** for AOM 2.0.

#. In the navigation tree on the left, click a function, for example, **Dashboard**.

Viewing Operation Logs
======================

AOM records operation logs of tasks such as installation/upgrade/uninstall/start/stop/restart related to UniAgent and other plug-ins. You can check the operation logs of related tasks.

Viewing UniAgent Operation Logs
-------------------------------

#. Log in to the AOM 2.0 console.
#. In the navigation pane on the left, choose **Settings** > **Global Settings**.
#. In the navigation tree on the left, choose **Collection Settings** > **Operation Logs**. The **UniAgent Logs** tab page is displayed by default.
#. Set criteria to search for historical tasks.

   -  Filter data by executor name.
   -  Filter historical tasks by date. Options: **Last hour**, **Last 6 hours**, **Last day**, **Last 3 days**, and **Custom**. You can query historical tasks of half a year at most.

#. Click a task ID. On the task details page that is displayed, click **View Log** to view UniAgent operation logs.

Viewing Plug-In Operation Logs
------------------------------

#. Log in to the AOM 2.0 console.
#. In the navigation pane on the left, choose **Settings** > **Global Settings**.
#. In the navigation tree on the left of the **Global Settings** page, choose **Collection Settings** > **Operation Logs**. On the displayed page, click the **Plug-in Logs** tab.
#. Set criteria to search for historical tasks.

   -  Filter data by executor name.
   -  Filter historical tasks by date. Options: **Last hour**, **Last 6 hours**, **Last day**, **Last 3 days**, and **Custom**. You can query historical tasks of half a year at most.

#. Click a task ID. On the task details page that is displayed, click **View Log** in the **Operation** column to view plug-in operation logs.
